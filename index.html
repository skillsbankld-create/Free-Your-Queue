Instructions

Four quick steps to free up real space in your week.

  1. Think of at least two things from your daily Queue that are no longer serving you — write them down as you go.
  2. Select the most appropriate method for removing each one.
  3. Choose a realistic timeline for removing the obligation, and enter it in the When column.
  4. Commit to acting on your plan.
ExtractRemove yourself by speaking directly to the person you're obligated to.
DelegateFind someone else to pass this to.
OutsourcePay or use a tool to get it done.
